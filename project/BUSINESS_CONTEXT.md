# Business Context

## Client Profile

**Client:** Confidential Digital Higher Education Provider  
**Operating Model:** Large-scale online and blended learning  
**Primary Concern:** Student success, retention and academic performance

## Why This Engagement Was Commissioned

A recent executive review identified several problems:

1. withdrawal is generally reported after it has happened rather than anticipated;
2. virtual-learning activity is reported separately from assessment performance;
3. module teams use inconsistent definitions of engagement;
4. management lacks a standard view of student progression across the teaching period;
5. current reporting does not clearly distinguish early warning indicators from descriptive hindsight.

The organisation wants to determine whether its existing data can support a more proactive Student Success analytics capability.

## Stakeholders

### Director of Student Success — Project Sponsor
Needs earlier visibility of disengagement and withdrawal risk so support teams can prioritise outreach appropriately.

### Head of Academic Performance
Needs reliable comparison of assessment behaviour, attainment and final outcomes across modules and presentations.

### Head of Digital Learning
Needs to understand how students interact with digital-learning resources and whether certain patterns indicate changing engagement.

### Student Retention Lead
Needs operational indicators that are understandable, timely and suitable for weekly monitoring.

### Data & Insight Lead
Needs reproducible logic, documented definitions, clear limitations and a solution that can be handed over.

## Decision Context

The executive team is considering investment in a formal student-success analytics capability.

The project should help determine:

- which metrics are reliable enough for operational reporting;
- what behavioural patterns appear before withdrawal or failure;
- what data limitations reduce confidence;
- which indicators could be monitored regularly;
- whether predictive work is justified;
- what governance safeguards would be needed before operational use.

## Important Analytical Considerations

The data includes different grains:

- modules and presentations;
- individual students;
- registrations;
- assessments;
- student assessment submissions;
- VLE resources;
- daily student-VLE interactions.

Teams must be careful not to duplicate students or inflate activity through incorrect joins.
